<objective>
Add the uniform two-counter pair to the Keeper at the single consume choke point + the 4 send sites,
add the label-less `keeper_l2_probe` heartbeat, and remove the legacy `keeper_reinject_dropped`
counter (REQ-3, REQ-4, REQ-6).

Purpose: The Keeper has no consumed/sent counters today — only the legacy `keeper_reinject_dropped`.
This plan introduces `keeper_messages_consumed` (one increment in `RecoveryConsumerBase.Consume`, the
choke point all 6 recovery consumers funnel through), `keeper_messages_sent` (via a shared `CountSent`
helper called after each successful Send in the 4 SENDING consumers — the 2 Delete consumers send
nothing), and `keeper_l2_probe` (once per `BitHealthLoop` tick, no labels). `KeeperMetrics` is
ctor-injected into the base consumer + `BitHealthLoop` via the existing `IMeterFactory` DI pattern.

Output: rewritten `KeeperMetrics`; `RecoveryConsumerBase` with consumed increment + `CountSent`;
6 consumer subclasses forwarding the new ctor arg (4 calling `CountSent`); `BitHealthLoop` probe count.
</objective>
