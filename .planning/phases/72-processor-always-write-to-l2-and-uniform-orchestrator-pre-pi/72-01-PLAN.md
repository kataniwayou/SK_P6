<objective>
Remove the Completed-only write asymmetry at its source: the processor's `OutputTail` always writes the `out:` blob for the three terminal outcomes (Completed/Failed/Cancelled), stamps a real `EntryId = dr.MessageId` on the Failed/Cancelled `Step*` records, and the `ProcessorPipeline` catch blocks + input-schema-validation failure route through `OutputTail` carrying `Data = validatedData` instead of bypassing the write.

Purpose: Make every terminal `Step*` result carry a real blob and `EntryId`, so the orchestrator (Plan 03) can read every outcome uniformly with no `outcome == Completed` branch.
Output: Modified `OutputTail.cs` + `ProcessorPipeline.cs` and extended-in-place `OutputTailFacts` + `PrePipelineFacts`.
</objective>
