<objective>
Add the three orchestrator-side keeper recovery consumers to the central Keeper service: `OrchestratorReinject`
(read `L2[out:entryId]` via STRLEN, clean-absent drop, re-inject the original Step-result to
`orchestrator-result` with the envelope `MessageId` overridden to the carried `messageId`), `OrchestratorInject`
(write `L2[data:messageId]` -> dispatch `EntryStepDispatch` -> delete `L2[out:entryId]` in strict order,
re-implementing the relocate body INLINE because the Keeper assembly cannot reference Orchestrator), and
`OrchestratorDelete` (delete `L2[out:entryId]` only). Wire all three onto the existing partitioned,
gate-open-only `keeper-recovery` endpoint and register them in the Keeper host.

Purpose: REQ-71-08/09/10 — the orchestrator's L2-op exhaustion escalations (read->REINJECT, write->INJECT,
delete->DELETE) land here, symmetric with the shipped Phase-70 processor recovery (D-04). The BIT health gate
governs orchestrator recovery too. Output: 3 new consumers + binder/Program wiring + 4 keeper test updates,
Keeper slice green, firewall intact.
</objective>
