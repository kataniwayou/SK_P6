<objective>
GREEN the readiness half of the shared surface: implement RedisReadyHealthCheck (bounded, never-throw)
and LatchedReadinessHealthCheck (sticky, no self-heal), then wire them into the ONE embedded listener so
ALL three consoles gain Redis on /health/ready and a hard latch on their required-dep readiness (bus + Redis)
with a single change point. This delivers HLTH-04/05/08 for orchestrator, keeper, and processor at once —
orchestrator needs no service-level plan because it inherits this and registers no watchdog.

Purpose: MassTransit/Redis auto-reconnect would silently return a pod to Ready after a sustained outage;
the latch defeats that self-heal so recovery is operator-restart only.
Output: two implemented readiness checks + the embedded-listener wiring.
</objective>
