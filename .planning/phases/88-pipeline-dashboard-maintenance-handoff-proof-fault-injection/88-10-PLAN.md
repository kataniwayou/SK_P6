Write the two documents the phase exists to produce: the runbook a maintenance department would
use, and the readiness verdict that says honestly whether this dashboard is fit to hand over.

The research left one open question for planning to settle: where the runbook lives. Authoring it
under `.planning/` is convenient and useless — a maintenance department would never find it there,
which is itself rubric question 6. This plan settles it: the runbook is authored at

thin pointer left at `.planning/phases/88-*/88-RUNBOOK.md` so the phase record stays
self-describing. That does not close the reachability gap — nothing in scope can — but it stops the
runbook itself from being one.

Purpose: satisfy HAND-01 and HAND-02, and prove by re-running Phase 87's own live proof that this
phase left the dashboards intact.
Output: the runbook, the pointer, the readiness verdict, and the final requirement statuses.
