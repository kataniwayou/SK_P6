<objective>
Build the runtime-only fault-injection mechanism that roadmap Success Criterion 5 names as a
deliverable, as a pure dot-sourceable library. Nothing like it exists: the two Phase-79 seams are
committed in `src/` but present in no `k8s/` manifest and on no live pod, and the only scripts that
reference them (`phase-67-harness.ps1`, `phase-79-falsify.ps1`) call `kubectl` zero times — their
mechanism is `docker compose up` interpolation, which cannot reach a k8s pod.

This plan authors the library only. It performs no cluster mutation of its own; the first live use
is plan 88-03's probe. Authoring it separately means the mechanism is reviewable and statically
gated before it is ever pointed at the live `skp` namespace.

Purpose: a left-armed seam poisons every later scenario in the phase AND every later resilience
sweep, and the failure presents as an inexplicable conservation violation. A blanket
`--replicas=1` restore silently dismantles the orchestrator HA the previous milestone shipped.
Both hazards are eliminated by construction here, once, rather than re-avoided per scenario.
Output: `scripts/lib/phase-88-cluster-ops.ps1`.
</objective>
