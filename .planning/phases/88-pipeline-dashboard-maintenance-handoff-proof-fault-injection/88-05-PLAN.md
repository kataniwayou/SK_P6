<!-- The scenario sequence every scenario obeys, and the artifact fields that prove it obeyed. -->

The mandatory sequence (roadmap "Design consequence", DISC-06). Skipping the re-baseline makes the
assertion void, because `set env` and `scale` both mint new pods with new `service_instance_id`
values and every aggregate briefly dips while they warm up:

   capture pre-arm baseline (recorded, not authoritative)
     -> arm (set env / scale / start the HTTP load)
     -> kubectl rollout status
     -> settle >= 150 s (two export cadences) with traffic flowing
     -> RE-CAPTURE the authoritative baseline        <- the DISC-01 band used for scoring
     -> trigger the fault
     -> capture after
     -> restore, disarm, ASSERT clean

Per-scenario artifact fields (extending the plan 88-04 single-proof schema):
  ScenarioId, Verdict, Lever, TargetTier,
  Moved (bool), CrossTalkHeld (bool), BaselineRecaptured (bool), StackRestored (bool),
  PanelResults[] one per asserted panel: { PanelId, PanelTitle, Regime, SeriesName,
      PredictedDirection, BandLow, BandHigh, BaselineValues[], BaselineStates[],
      AfterValues[], AfterStates[], Moved, ConsecutiveSamplesOutside, PanelStateAfter,
      LegendNamesBaseline[], LegendNamesAfter[] },
  CrossTalkPanels[] one per declared cross-talk panel: { PanelId, BandLow, BandHigh,
      AfterValues[], StayedPut, MaxExcursion },
  PreArmBaselineValues[], RolloutOldInstanceIds[], RolloutNewInstanceIds[], RolloutUtc,
  ReplicasBefore, ReplicasAfter, ReplicasRestored, SeamVarsAfter[], SeamVarsClean,
  ImagesUnchanged,
  BaselineWindowStart/End, AfterWindowStart/End, SubWindowSeconds, SubWindowCount,
  ViewportWidth, ViewportHeight, RateIntervalSeconds, OldestSampleUtc,
  DiagnosticQueryValue, DiagnosticAgreesWithPanel, ScreenshotPaths[],
  UnevaluablePanels[], HumanSummary

Scoring rules (fixed; no scenario may redefine them):
  Moved      = the after-window value lies outside the band, in the predicted direction, for
               >= 2 CONSECUTIVE 60-second samples. At a 60 s sampling interval a single excursion
               is indistinguishable from a sampling artifact.
  NoData     = a first-class panel STATE. Where NoData is the predicted direction it counts as
               MOVED, not as an unread panel.
  StayedPut  = the cross-talk panel's value remained inside its band for the entire fault window.
               A cross-talk panel that drifts outside is a FINDING, recorded, not suppressed.
  Verdict    Fail beats Inconclusive. A panel read successfully whose value did not move in the
             predicted direction is Fail. A panel that could not be READ at all is Inconclusive.
             A false restore claim is Fail and exits 65.

WEB-01 traffic shape: sustained concurrency of about 10 parallel requesters against non-health
routes on `http://localhost:8080` for at least 180 seconds. The collector drops `/health/` routes,
and `http_server_active_requests` / `kestrel_active_connections` / `kestrel_queued_connections` are
GAUGES sampled at the 60-second export cadence — a request that begins and ends between two
exports is never recorded at all, so a burst would leave panel 14 reading 0 at every tick.

WEB-02: gated on the probe's `Safe5xxFound` / `Safe5xxRoute`. If the probe found no safe 5xx, this
scenario is Dropped and panel 13 is recorded in the accepted-unproven register. Do NOT substitute
the redis dependency-outage route: it wipes L2 (redis has no PVC) and the Phase-86 hard readiness
latch means recovery costs a WebApi pod restart.
