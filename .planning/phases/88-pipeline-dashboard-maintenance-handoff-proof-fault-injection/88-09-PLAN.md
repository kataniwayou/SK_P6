Six questions, answered yes / partial / no, per panel. NO weights and NO aggregate score — the
blocking-gap list is the verdict, and plan 88-10 states it.

  1 Nameable       Can someone who did not build this say what the panel measures from the title
                   and tooltip alone? Test: the description states what it shows, what healthy looks
                   like, and when to investigate. Evidence: the panel `description` (all 31
                   non-row panels carry one, enforced by lint rule S16).
  2 Falsifiable    Does "is this normal?" have an answer? Test: a DISC-01 baseline band exists and
                   is written into the runbook. Evidence: analyzer-reports/phase-88-BASE-01.json.
  3 Discriminating Has the panel been seen in >= 2 states? Test: DISC-02 proven, or present in the
                   HAND-04 register. Evidence: the roll-up's `observedStates[]`.
  4 Actionable     Is there a named next action for the abnormal state? Test: a HAND-01 runbook row
                   exists citing the proving scenario. Evidence: the runbook authored in plan 88-10.
  5 Non-misleading Does the default rendering mean what it appears to mean? Test: thresholds, axis
                   auto-scale and `or vector(0)` guards each checked. Expect fail or partial on at
                   least six panels.
  6 Reachable      Can maintenance actually open it? Test: a documented access path. Known gap:
                   loopback `kubectl port-forward` only, anonymous Viewer, `admin`/`admin` in the
                   manifest — an accepted dev posture, out of scope to fix, but a BLOCKING gap for
                   a genuine handoff and it must be stated as one.

Register entry shape, borrowed from `87-FINDINGS.md`'s fixed headings:
  **Asked for** (the panel's DISC-02 obligation)
  **What actually exists** (the observed states, from the roll-up)
  **What shipped instead** (the log-corroboration route maintenance should use)
  **Why it is legitimate rather than a shortfall** (the user lock, or the probe result)
  **Measured evidence:** citing the scenario artifact and the roll-up row.
