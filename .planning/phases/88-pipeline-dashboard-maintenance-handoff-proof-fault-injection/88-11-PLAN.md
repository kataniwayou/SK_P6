<objective>
Have a human read the two handoff deliverables and either sign them off or send them back.

Practicality is a judgement, not an assertion. The phase's validation strategy records three
manual-only verifications for exactly this reason: whether a runbook is usable by a team that did
not build the system, whether the readiness verdict is honest about its gaps, and whether panel 7's
accepted-unproven treatment tells maintenance something actionable. No automated check can answer
those.

Phase 87 carried forward a note that its human checkpoint was signed off by instruction rather than
by a reviewer walking the panels. That is recorded rather than papered over, and this checkpoint
exists so Phase 88 does not repeat it silently. If the sign-off is again by instruction, it must say
so.

Purpose: close HAND-01 and HAND-02 with attributed human review.
Output: a Sign-off section appended to `88-HANDOFF-VERDICT.md`.
</objective>
